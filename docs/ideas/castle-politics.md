# Castle politics (next update)

Recorded 11 October 2026 from the owner's request; not built yet. The territory phases, offerings, reputation and
grievance ledger from the same request were built in 0.11.0 ([domain-and-influence](../systems/domain-and-influence.md)).

## The owner's words

> Starting in phase 3 there are some inter map politics where castles could have territories and expand those
> territories by going to war with other villages, castles, and you can choose to help a side or attack both sides
> to build influence with certain territories.

Also decided: seven large regions; your home region is your main territory (your rule cannot leave it, which leaves
the world for the opposition); one Crystal of Life in each of the other six regions. Castle politics was chosen to
come after the territory rework.

## What exists to build on

- Realms (factions) own subregions and go to war with neighbours; armies march once a year, deplete garrisons,
  leave battlefields; mercenary contracts ([realms-and-memory](../systems/realms-and-memory.md)).
- Your home region holds two or three realms with 4–12 castles and their villages (0.11 world generation).
- Castles can swear fealty to an adult dragon; every realm's fear, trust, hostility and heat show in Reputation.

## Proposal (Claude's, to discuss)

1. **Lordships.** Each castle holds the farms and villages nearest it inside its realm (a lord, a banner colour, a
   small territory drawn as a thin ink border on the map, only from phase 3 and only in your home region at first).
2. **Feuds.** Each year a lord may press a claim on a neighbouring village, a free village or another lord's castle:
   a feud with a named attacker and defender, a muster, a date. Unanswered, the stronger side usually wins and the
   border moves (villages change hands, are sacked or burned).
3. **Your choices when a feud starts** (a card and an Aims entry): help the attacker (a raid scene on the defender
   where their soldiers fight you), help the defender (a defend scene like the outlaw raids), attack both (break
   both armies in the field: fear from both, the region weakened), or stay out. Helping raises trust or fear with
   that lordship, may make the winner swear fealty, and gives the loser a grudge in the ledger.
4. **Reputation by lordship**: the Realms tab grows a row per lord in your territory (trust, fear, who they owe).
5. **Opposition space**: feuds beyond your home region happen without you and feed the great struggle (the Warlord
   recruits from broken lordships; a crystal's realm grows desperate).
