Moving light on a mountain

A test for HoardBound's map symbols. Every symbol is only an outline: no shading is drawn. A sun lights them, and the shade, the etching and the shadows follow it. Try one mountain up close, or a small 3D map you can turn.

Drag on the mountain to move the sun: left and right swing it around, up and down raise and lower it.

At map size: your three game mountains (outline only, their painted shading removed) and the sketch at half size, all under one sun. Some are mirrored: with computed light a mirrored copy is still lit from the right side.

Sun stays

With the view: the sun keeps its place on the screen while you turn the map, so the lit side is always the same side. On the map: the sun stays put in the world, so turning the map changes which side of each symbol faces it. The compass shows north and where the sun is (closer to the middle is higher).

How it works

  • The page guesses each symbol's shape from its outline: the further a point is from the edge, the closer it bulges toward you, so ridges run down from each peak. The Shape sliders change how deep and how sharp it is.
  • Every point then knows which way it faces, so it knows how much sun it gets. Cel shading turns that into flat tones, a dark edge on the shadow side that grows as the sun swings away, and thin highlights on the sunny side. Etching turns it into lines instead: more lines, longer lines, and cross-hatching where it is darkest.
  • Etching lines run along the sun's direction, bent toward the slope by "Lines follow". Each line has a fixed place, so as the sun moves the lines turn and fade in or out instead of flickering.

What it would mean for the game

  • You would draw an outline and a flat fill, like your third sketch without the hatching. The light adds the shading, at any drawing size.
  • Each kind of symbol is lit once per frame and copied for every spot, so a whole forest costs the same as one tree. Mirrored copies are fine again, since the light is worked out after mirroring.
  • Still open: where the sun comes from in the game (a fixed afternoon light, the time of day as you fly, or turning the map), and whether you want to mark extra ridge lines the outline does not show.
